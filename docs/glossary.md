# Glossary

## A

- **ADR**: Architecture Decision Record.
- **ABAC**: Attribute-Based Access Control.
- **API**: Application Programming Interface.

## B

- **Break-glass**: Emergency elevated access with audit.

## C

- **Claim**: Insurance claim for services rendered.
- **COCOMO**: Constructive Cost Model (effort estimation).

## D

- **DICOM**: Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine.
- **DRYness**: Measure of code duplication (scc metric).

## E

- **EHR**: Electronic Health Record.
- **Encounter**: A clinical visit/interaction.

## F

- **FHIR**: Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources.

## H

- **HIPAA**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.
- **HL7**: Health Level Seven (messaging standard).

## I

- **ICD-10**: International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision.

## K

- **K8s**: Kubernetes.

## M

- **MFA**: Multi-Factor Authentication.

## O

- **OLTP**: Online Transaction Processing.

## P

- **PHI**: Protected Health Information.
- **Pydantic**: Data validation library.

## R

- **RBAC**: Role-Based Access Control.

## S

- **SLO**: Service Level Objective.
- **SSO**: Single Sign-On.

## T

- **Terraform**: Infrastructure as Code tool.
