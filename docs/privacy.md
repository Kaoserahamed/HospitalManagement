# Privacy and Data Protection

## 1. Principles

- Data minimization.
- Purpose limitation.
- Storage limitation.
- Integrity and confidentiality.
- Accountability.

## 2. Data Collection

- Collect only data necessary for care and operations.
- Obtain consent where required.
- Document purpose for each data element.

## 3. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

- Right of access: export patient data.
- Right to rectification: correct inaccurate data.
- Right to erasure: delete where legally permitted.
- Right to portability: machine-readable export.
- Right to object: opt out of non-essential processing.

## 4. Retention

- Clinical records: retained per legal/clinical requirements.
- Logs: 90 days (configurable).
- Backups: 30 days rolling.
- Deletion requests tracked and audited.

## 5. Cross-Border Transfers

- Data residency requirements respected.
- Standard contractual clauses where applicable.

## 6. Third Parties

- Data processing agreements (DPAs) with vendors.
- Minimum necessary sharing.
