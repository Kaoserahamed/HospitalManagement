# Functional Requirements - Hospital Management System

## 1. User Management
- **User Registration & Authentication**
  - Register new users (patients, doctors, admins)
  - Login/logout with secure authentication
  - Role-based access control (RBAC)
  - Password reset functionality

## 2. Patient Management
- **Patient Registration**
  - Register new patients with demographic information
  - Assign unique patient ID
  - Store medical history
  - Update patient information

- **Patient Records**
  - View patient medical history
  - Track patient visits
  - Store vital signs and measurements

## 3. Doctor Management
- **Doctor Profile**
  - Manage doctor profiles (specialization, qualifications, schedule)
  - Track doctor availability
  - Manage department assignments

- **Doctor Scheduling**
  - Set working hours and availability
  - Manage consultation slots

## 4. Appointment Management
- **Booking System**
  - Book appointments with available doctors
  - View available time slots
  - Cancel/reschedule appointments
  - Appointment status tracking (scheduled, completed, cancelled)

- **Appointment Notifications**
  - Confirmation notifications
  - Reminder notifications

## 5. Department Management
- **Department Operations**
  - Create and manage hospital departments
  - Assign doctors to departments
  - Track department resources

## 6. Medical Records
- **Electronic Health Records (EHR)**
  - Create and update medical records
  - Record diagnoses and treatments
  - Prescription management
  - Lab test results tracking

## 7. Prescription Management
- **Prescription Creation**
  - Doctors can create prescriptions
  - Link prescriptions to appointments
  - Track medication history

## 8. Billing & Payments
- **Billing Operations**
  - Generate bills for appointments and services
  - Track payment status
  - Payment history tracking
  - Generate invoices

## 9. Dashboard & Reporting
- **Admin Dashboard**
  - Overview of hospital operations
  - Patient statistics
  - Appointment analytics
  - Revenue reports

- **Doctor Dashboard**
  - View scheduled appointments
  - Access patient records
  - Manage availability

- **Patient Dashboard**
  - View appointment history
  - Access medical records
  - View prescriptions and bills
